Welcome to The Sorting Game!

Is that its official name? I don’t know!

I adapted this from Thomas Colthurst’s original game, which was published under Creative Commons Share Alike. In his version, there are 45 categories with 45 items each.

In this version, there are 150+ total categories, a subset of which will be randomly selected upon generating a puzzle. Most of the categories have more than 20 items (and some categories, many more!), so even if you get the same category across multiple games, you are likely to encounter new items.

With over 6,000 total items, it’s inevitable that some items could logically fit into multiple categories. For these, my apologies!
